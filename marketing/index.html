What it does

A QA team that works while you sleep

Not a codegen tool bolted onto your CI — agents that behave like QA engineers: they look at your real app, reason about what matters, and keep working after the first test fails.

Live exploration, not guesswork

Point an agent at a real environment and watch it navigate live, then review test cases designed only from what it actually saw — pause, stop, or redirect it anytime.

A teammate you talk to

Ask about coverage gaps, flaky tests, or what to test next in a real console — and let agents work proactively, surfacing what they find before you ask.

Failures that fix themselves

A locator drifts, a test fails — the agent diagnoses the root cause, proposes a resilient fix, and opens a pull request the moment a human approves it.

Proactive, not just reactive

Outpost runs agents on a schedule — scanning for coverage gaps and failure patterns — and hands you a ready-made console session the moment something's worth a look.
